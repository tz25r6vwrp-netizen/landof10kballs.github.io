About Us

🌲 Born on Minnesota Lakes

Land of 10K Balls started where you'd expect — on the water. We’re based in Mendota Heights, MN and spend our summers recovering premium golf balls from private courses and lakes around the Twin Cities.

Every ball is hand-washed, sorted, and graded by actual golfers. No machines. No bulk tumblers. If it’s not 100% playable, it doesn’t make the cut.
